# Formats by niche: beat sheets

Timings are for a typical 30–60 s vertical piece. Each format was seen repeatedly across finished agency work; client names have been generalised to the niche.

---

## 1. Real-estate listing: land, building or villa
Length 40–70 s, 10–25 cuts/min, voice-over plus corporate real-estate music.

| t | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–1.5 s | Drone push-in on the plot, or the presenter greeting "حياكم / اليوم نستعرض لكم…" with a whip. Optional "العرض حصري" white title |
| 1.5–4 s | **Map mode**: tint the frame blue or violet, draw a glowing gold, orange or cyan outline on the plot or building, and pop the area number in 3D ("412 م²", "6073") along the plot edge, with dimension arrows |
| 4–20 s | Location proof: 3D road names laid in perspective ("طريق سلطانة", "الدائري الثاني"), an arc or route line to landmarks (Haram, train station, malls, مسجد قباء), a "3 دقائق" distance arc, landmark polygons outlined in orange |
| 20–45 s | Walkthrough: exterior at dusk with lights, a gimbal interior with small white captions naming each feature (غرفة للعاملة, كاميرات مراقبة, 10 سنوات تأمين) plus a line icon; a big white 3D feature word |
| 45–55 s | Price in a big glow ("2,000,000 شامل الضريبة", "880.000 ⃁"), or a frosted-glass spec sheet listing each plot (area, frontage, rent, licence number) over the blurred plot |
| last 2–3 s | QR code (local ad-licence authority) + licence number + phone, OR a white brand card (logo + phone + handle), then dim to black |

Variants to export: with and without the broker logo ("مع لوجو" / white-label for each broker), a new phone number ("الرقم الجديد"), with and without music, and 9:16 plus 16:9.
Big launches (major developers): full After Effects drone VFX with multiple buildings labelled, a hologram scan on the 3D render, glowing building edges at dusk and the developer logo in a tracked glass plate.
Construction progress: music only with stat overlays ("عدد عمال الصب: 150 عامل"), an orange site outline, a speed-ramped drone and the logo watermark.
Holy-city payoff: listings near a landmark end on it at dusk with a respectful caption naming the site.

## 2. Clinic: educational
Length 25–55 s.
- **Hook**: a question or myth as a big kashida stack or a top title ("كويس للفينيرز ولا لا", "قال بيـــقلك"), with a defocus-in or a walk-in.
- **Body**: doctor to camera with the brand pill captions. Each term gets an icon or 3D tooth. Add a full-screen explainer card (brand gradient, 3D tooth cross-section, "حركة دائرية"), AI or stock pain stills, a before/after grid, and a strike-through on myths.
- **Close**: an engagement question ("وأنت شو أكتر أكلة خايف تخسرها؟") or the phone pill and logo lower third, then fade.

## 3. Clinic: offer ad
Length 15–35 s. The presenter interrupts ("لحظة!" with a red ✗), then:
1. Service buttons appear (glass or brand pills: زراعة / هوليود سمايل / فينير).
2. Offer items stack as pills.
3. The price in a glowing ring ("20000 ليرة" / "7900 TRY"), with warranty or certificate proof.
4. A location pin (naming the district).
5. A WhatsApp pill plus the number.
6. The end card: logo, phone and patients' country flags.

Also make language versions (AR, ENG, TR, Farsi) and country-targeted cuts that pop the country's flag ("إذا كنت من الجزائر").

## 4. Clinic: skits and games
Reception or call-centre comedy (a circular caller avatar top-left), doctors rating treatments with score cards (5/8/10), a recurring patient character, and emoji hook titles ("كيف صارت بعد!! 😂").

## 5. Restaurant: process ASMR
Length 12–25 s, 22–45 cuts/min, cut to the beat, loud master, no VO.
Build the montage from **macro process** shots (spit shaving, sauce drizzle, fryer, cheese pull), POV hands, and low angles with heater glow. Include branded packaging in shot, then the hero product held to the lens (shallow depth of field) or a big-bite reaction, then the storefront sign. Add one title at most, or none.

## 6. Restaurant: presenter formats
- **Challenge / budget**: "معي 2000 ليرة وأبغى اتغدى فيها" with a running balance number counting down per item (715 → 580 → 420…) and green-tinted banknotes.
- **Street quiz**: "5000 ليرة إذا جاوبت صح", with the money number in green.
- **Street game**: a freeze frame, participants in white sticker outlines, the rule title in a blue outline, then a zoom-blur into the action; the payoff is a free meal.
- **Vox-pop / tasting**: a handheld lav mic, strangers' reactions, a punch-zoom on the laugh, handwritten rating cards.
- **Cashier micro (6–8 s)**: "بايش تنصحني؟", then the menu with glowing prices.
- **Offer skit**: the customer orders, the cashier pitches the deal, emoji stickers multiply, a "+330" counter, the terms in green numerals.
- **Menu item**: an emoji label title ("مطبق تونه 🐟"), then a tasting reaction.
- **Product drop**: pixelated until the reveal; "نداء 📣 عاااجل" kinetic hook, then "ترى موجود لفترة محدودة".
- **Series / episodic**: a countdown to opening ("باقي 15 يوم"), "الجزء الثاني", "لا تنسوا تتابعوا الصفحة".
- **Price ad**: the old price in red with a ✗, the new price on a yellow-green starburst, and the offer text in white bold serif with a black stroke in the top third ("25 Ocak'ta %25 indirim").
- **Close**: "تعال جربنا بنفسك" plus the area name, or the phone number big plus the location line, or a comment keyword ("اكتب مهتم ويوصلك المنيو").

## 7. Study abroad / education
- **Hook**: an objection question ("فاتك تسجيل سبتمبر؟", "قديش بتكلف…؟"), a neon "SEPT." behind the head, or a cinematic spotlight silhouette.
- **Body**: a presenter on campus or on a brand set; bilingual captions; the flag full-bleed behind them with the country name; fees in big numbers or neon green; 3D glossy icon tiles; a navy grid board with the presenter inset; cutouts (graduate, passport, licence card); a location pin card.
- **Close**: "تواصل معنا حتى نراجع حالتك", "احجز مقعدك", or "So what are you waiting for?". Logo build at the end.
- Turkmen, Turkish, English and Arabic versions are all common.

## 8. Coach / expert / personal brand
- **Stage or long-take talks**: the darkened freeze hook frame, a persistent navy topic box, white captions with yellow key words and a name/handle watermark. Around 4 cuts/min; the captions carry the pace.
- **Podcast Q&A series**: the question in a coloured box at the top, answer captions on white or grey pills, one long take, many clips cut from one session.
- **Education reel** (talk-only creator client): an orange hook bar with a split screen (speaker top, movie or meme bottom), proof screenshots circled in red, comparison columns accumulating, and "اكتب كلمة X في التعليقات".
- **Motion-graphics heavy**: neon 3D numbers and prices, dark grid rooms with glowing 3D icons, product renders on number outlines, flag swaps, strike-and-replace text ("التجارة مش للأغنياء" → "للأذكياء"). 15–20 s.
- **Listicle metaphor**: "السلاح ⚔️ الأول", numbered blue circles 1-2-3, numbered neon boxes.
- **Creator packaging assets**: an alpha lower third (name bar), a channel outro and animated question cards rendered once and reused.

## 9. SaaS / B2B
Kinetic glass typography around the presenter, the key word in the brand colour, full-screen brand boards with line icons, glass UI cards with counting stats ("$61,372", "5,000 users"), and feature pills (Email / WhatsApp / Website). Partner logos in the corners. CTA "أنشئ حسابك وابدأ هلق / البداية مجانية" or "Register via the link below". Make 9:16 and 16:9 versions. Expect A/B tests of the caption style.

## 10. App / consumer brand ads
- UGC-ad structure: problem → emotional device (red tint flash, B&W bad outcome, AI scenario still) → the brand pop (app icon scale bounce plus tagline) → proof (news split screen, DM screenshot) → CTA ("aşağıdaki butona tıkla", "اطلبوه عن طريق الرسائل") → white end card with App Store and Google Play badges.
- Comparison skits: a white divider line drawing down, other options in white vs the brand's list in green; or two actors with label boxes.
- Games and quizzes as disguised ads (the final answer reveals the brand).
- Office-culture "POV" relatable content with the product placed in frame.
- Portfolio ads for freelancers or voice artists: work thumbnails sliding along the edge, tilted phone-frame collage, waveform under the captions, one colour per demo style.

## 11. Brand films, events, fashion
Music only. Open on a drone or from black; atelier craft close-ups; split-screen stacked panels; outfit-change transitions (walk behind a tree or pole, come out in a new look); Google-Earth zoom into the venue; an animated logo end card on white, black or a brand background, then fade. Also cut **BTS versions** (4:5 and 9:16, with the film stacked over the Premiere timeline) for the agency page.

## 12. Seasonal greetings
Five to fifteen seconds. Either an After Effects template (3D mosque and lanterns, a paper-cut Eid scene, a Kaaba illustration, a luxury 8-point star) with calligraphy and the logo, or the owner or chairman greeting from a desk with a name plate, fading in and out from black. Make Arabic and Turkish versions, plus personalised name pills in batches.

## 13. Agency's own marketing (We Viral)
- **Portfolio grid**: a navy background with concentric thin rings, a 2x2 grid of client reels playing, the one being discussed outlined in glowing orange-red, the title "تصوير ومونتاج / ريلز احترافي / في اسطنبول", and an end card with the number.
- **Package ad**: a darkened grid behind, 3 stacked navy cards with orange-red outlines (package tiers with reel counts and prices in TL/USD), a footer with the agency's IG handle and WhatsApp number. Export TL, USD and no-music versions.
- **Carousel ad**: reel cards slide right to left with orange claim lines (years of experience, reel count delivered, studio location), then "احجز جلستك الآن".
- **Niche showcase**: the grid filled with one niche's reels ("ريلز مطاعم").
- **BTS 4:5**: three vertical slices of BTS footage and a white band with the red "We Viral" wordmark and a production-volume claim line.
- **Client showcase posts**: 15 s, silent (music added in-app), a 2x2 grid of one client's reels and the red "We Viral" logo.
