# House style: look, type, motion, audio

These are the recurring visual and audio decisions observed across 1,088 finished renders.

## Canvas and safe zones
- The canvas is 1080x1920. Keep text inside x 80–1000 and y 250–1500. The top ~220 px is covered by platform UI and the bottom ~400 px by the caption/CTA UI on IG and TikTok.
- **Running captions** sit at about 62–72% of frame height, at chest level under the face. On wide or talking shots they may sit at about 55%.
- **Hook or topic titles** sit in the top third (y ≈ 280–600) or centred on a darkened frame.
- **Logo bug**: small, top-left, top-right or top-centre, persistent (common on clinics, education brands). Alternatively a **credit watermark** at bottom-centre ("إنتاج: <agency name> + phone", "<creator name> @handle") at 40–60% opacity.
- **Phone pill**: at the lower-middle during the CTA, often persistent through the last 5–10 s.

## Typography
- **Arabic** fonts seen in projects: Lama Sans, GE Dinar One, Somar (ArbFONTS), Cairo, Greta Arabic, Swissra, Expo Arabic, DIN Next LT Arabic, Instagram Sans Arabic, Thmanyah Sans (the We Viral site font). Captions are always a heavy weight (Bold, Black, ExtraBold).
- **Latin** fonts: Montserrat or Nunito Sans (Bold/Black) for captions, BDO Grotesk, Urbanist, Ginóra Sans, Lenos, Moderna Sans, and Bebas/Anton-style condensed caps for Turkish retail.
- **Kashida stretching** (تطويل) is a signature: words are stretched to justify stacked lines or to add emphasis (الفـــينير، خصـــم، بتخـــاف). Use it for hook stacks and key words, not for running captions.
- **Letter-repeat emphasis** in the spoken style: "مرررررة", "عاااجل".
- **Mixed scales**: a key word is 1.5–3x bigger than the words around it. Stacked lines use different weights and sizes, and sometimes italics.

## Caption systems (pick one per client and keep it)
1. **Brand pill**: white text on a rounded brand-colour pill (common on coach/creator, clinic and restaurant clients).
2. **Key-word colour**: white bold text with a soft black shadow; only the key word is in the brand colour (common on coach/creator and clinic clients).
3. **White label, dark text**: navy or brand text on a white pill, with the key word bolder (common on coach/creator and clinic clients).
4. **Glassmorphism**: white text in a frosted translucent rounded box with backdrop blur (common on clinic and SaaS clients).
5. **Word-by-word build**: one word at a time, big and condensed, building a stacked sentence with mixed sizes, occasional colour or italic words and a highlight box (common on clinic and coach clients).
6. **Karaoke bar**: an ALL-CAPS line on a translucent navy bar that fills word by word (common on study-abroad clients).
7. **Bilingual**: the main-language line big, with the second language small underneath in white or in an outlined pill (common on SaaS, study-abroad and clinic clients).
8. **Floating words**: soft-glow words placed in the negative space above the head or anchored to background architecture (common on study-abroad and clinic clients).

Numbers from the SRT library (9,428 cues): median cue 1.2 s (p10 0.57, p90 1.95), 3 words (p90 5), about 16 characters, **always one line**.

## Colour logic
- One **brand accent** per client, plus white, plus black shadows. The client bible lists each accent.
- **Money green** is for prices, savings, profits and "free" (مجاني). **Red** is for wrong, old price, danger or urgency, with a red ✗, strike-through or a full-frame red tint flash. **Yellow** is for highlight numbers and key words on neutral brands.
- Country and flag colours go on destination words (من تركيا إلى **سوريا** in the flag's green and red).
- **Blue or purple map mode** for real estate: tint the whole drone frame deep blue or violet, then draw the subject in glowing gold or orange (plots), cyan (buildings) or yellow dashes (boundaries).

## Graphic vocabulary (reuse these; they are the agency's toolkit)
- **Line icons** (white or neon) drawn on beside the head, one per point: tooth, clock, money, document, chart, phone, org chart, map pin.
- **3D glossy icons and Apple-style 3D emoji** pop in with a scale overshoot: tooth, shield-check, money stack, house, taxi, pregnant woman, 3D ✓ or ✗.
- **Neon glow numbers and words** ("$3000" green, "$1000" red, "3X" blue, "2024"), sometimes placed behind the presenter with a mask so the text sits *behind* the head (common on study-abroad and coach clients).
- **Glass cards**: rounded or arch-shaped frosted panels holding a word, a photo or UI.
- **Full-screen explainer boards**: a dark navy or teal perspective grid, a brand gradient, a peach or cream gradient, or a white wall with window-shadow texture. The presenter sits in a rounded inset at the top and the title builds line by line.
- **Split screen**: speaker top and visual bottom (movie, meme, news, UI), both as rounded cards on black. Or two columns ✓ vs ✗ with lists that accumulate.
- **Proof overlays**: profile screenshots circled in red by hand, DM or comment screenshots stacking, "Replying to…" comment stickers, view-count thumbnails.
- **Cutouts with a thick white sticker outline** on freeze frames and thumbnail-style cards.
- **AI-generated stills** for scenarios you can't film (a patient worried at the door, a Saudi client on the phone, a cat alone at home, a future building on a plot). Real-estate AI renders carry the disclaimer "تم توليد هذه اللقطة بالذكاء الاصطناعي".
- **Money rain**, confetti bursts, sparks and floating "?" for the closing question.
- **Map pins, route arcs, dimension arrows** with the metres or area written in perspective on the plot.
- **Price treatment**: the old price in red with a strike or ✗, the new price big on a yellow-green starburst, or the price inside a thin glowing ring.

## Motion
- Text enters with a fade and position move per character or word, a scale overshoot, blur-in, a typewriter effect or a highlighter wipe (the Essential Graphics text animators in the project libraries).
- **Transitions**, most common first: hard cut, then whip or directional blur, radial zoom blur, light leak or light streak, glass or arc wipe, diagonal white wipe, Gaussian-blur dissolve, and cross dissolve (soft or emotional moments only). Reserve glitch for tech and event pieces.
- **Punch-ins** of about 110–125% on jump cuts and emphasis words. Add a slow push-in on long single takes.
- **Tint flashes** on emphasis: red (urgency), brand colour, B&W for "the bad outcome".
- **Freeze frames** for hooks and game rules, and for thumbnail-style start and end cards.
- **Speed ramps and hyperlapse** for real-estate walkthroughs and construction progress.

## End cards (house standard)
- **White or light-grey card**: logo centred, then phone, handle, website or App Store and Google Play badges, sometimes a row of country flags or city and phone lines revealed one by one. Hold for 1.5–2.5 s, then fade to black.
- **Brand-colour card** (navy, purple or orange depending on the client's accent) with the logo and contact details.
- **On footage**: the presenter with open arms, a big phone pill and the logo bug, then dim to black.
- **Long-form funnel card**: a YouTube thumbnail card with "اترك تعليق ليصلك الرابط" (common on coach/creator clients).
- For the agency's own ads: the navy concentric-ring end card with a glow "احجز جلستك الآن" and the WhatsApp number.

## Audio
- Voice chain: Adobe Podcast Enhance Speech v2 at about 50% strength, then DeNoise, parametric EQ, Vocal Enhancer or a compressor, then a hard limiter.
- Music choice by genre:
  - **Talking/educational:** lofi or chill talking beds (15/30/60 s edits).
  - **Real estate:** corporate "real-estate" loops.
  - **Retail and food:** funky or upbeat.
  - **Skits:** comedy soundtrack.
  - **Brand films:** cinematic cello, violin or piano.
  - **Events:** EDM.
  - **Religious or conservative clients:** nasheed or vocal-and-daf beds with no instruments.
- Add sound effects on pops: whoosh on whips, pop or click on icons, a riser into reveals, an attention "ding" on "السؤال الحقيقي".
- Master to **−14 LUFS integrated with −1 dBTP true peak**. Deliver music-only pieces at around −12 to −10 but never clipping. Make **silent "No Music" exports** when the client adds trending audio in-app.

## Colour and picture
- **Do not grade.** Footage is delivered graded. Lumetri is only for matching and exposure fixes.
- Stabilise handheld shots with Warp Stabilizer or Topaz when they are shaky. Upscale with Topaz when the source is weak.
- Replace or key backgrounds (Ultra Key) when the set is poor: night sky, city, AI street.
